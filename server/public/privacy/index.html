Privacy

myPod is a music player by CappyAI Ltd. We keep only what we need to run the beta.

Updated 21 November 2026

Your music

  • myPod plays your music through Spotify or Apple Music. Your login stays on your Mac, and we never see it.
  • myPod never changes your playlists or liked songs. It saves no music and no covers.

Your account

  • To turn on myPod, you give your email. Our server keeps your email, an account number, a code for each Mac, and the dates you joined and last used myPod.
  • If you wait for a beta spot, we write to you only about your spot and the myPod launch.

Usage stats

  • myPod sends usage stats: setup steps, songs and minutes played, features used, and crashes. They help us fix myPod and choose what to build next.
  • They carry your account number, never song, artist or playlist names. Our stats service keeps them with your country and city.
  • To turn them off, paste this in Terminal:defaults write com.cappyai.mypod analytics.on -bool false

Report a Problem

  • A report carries your note, your account number, and basic facts about your Mac and myPod. It carries your email only if you ask us to write back.

Who helps us

  • Trusted services help us run our server and website, send mail and keep usage stats. Some of them are in the US. Write to us for the list.

Your data

  • We use it to run the beta and make myPod better. We keep it only while we need it for that. We do not sell it.
  • To see, fix or delete your data, write to feedback@getmypod.app. We answer within a month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
